Good to know

A few quick answers.

Can I walk in?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is available. Booking ahead is the best way to get your preferred time and artist.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?

Not quite. The form sends us your preferred details, and we'll contact you by email to confirm the appointment.

How do I choose the right service?

Each service page has a short description and estimated duration. If you're considering a color change, start with the complimentary color consultation.

How long should I set aside?

Timing varies by service. Check the duration on its detail page, and allow a little extra time for a first visit or a bigger change.
